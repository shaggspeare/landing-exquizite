(01) Latest update

New in Calleo

01

Listen. Write. Two new ways to practice.

Beyond flashcards, quizzes, and matching, Calleo now tests you the way real conversations do. Listening mode plays the word aloud and you identify it, training your ear, not just your eyes. Writing mode flips it around: you see the translation and type the word from memory, building real recall instead of multiple-choice guessing.

Build a set in seconds, not minutes

Typing out word lists is the boring part, so skip it. With Quick Generate, type a topic like “restaurant” or “travel”, scan a photo of a menu, sign or page, or paste in an article, lyrics or a chat, and AI builds a ready-to-use vocabulary set. (08) FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01Do I need an account to use Calleo?

No! Start learning immediately with guest mode. Create an account later to sync across devices.

02How many languages are supported?

Over 40 languages for both learning content and app interface.

03Does the app work offline?

Yes, the iOS app supports offline access to your downloaded word sets.

04Can I use Calleo on my computer?

Absolutely! The web app offers a desktop-optimized experience.

05Is my data secure?

Your sets live in our cloud database, and each account can only read its own data. Words and topics you send to the AI go to OpenAI to produce translations, transcriptions and examples; your email and account data do not. In guest mode nothing leaves your device except those AI requests.

06Can I share my word sets?

Yes! Generate a shareable link for any set. Others can view and copy it to their collection.

07What game modes does Calleo offer?

Six: Flashcards, Match, Quiz, Fill in the Blank, Listening, and Writing. Listening plays a word aloud for you to identify; Writing has you type the word from memory.

08Can I import word lists from Wordwall?

Yes! Import your existing Wordwall sets directly into Calleo and start practicing right away.

09What is the CEFR Learn path?

Learn is a guided curriculum spanning all six CEFR levels, A1 through C2. Finish a Full run of all six practice modes to unlock the next focused set.
